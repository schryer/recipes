Yeast and buckwheat give poffertjes their light, spongy and slightly
chewy texture. Let the batter rise properly, and flip them as soon as the
bottoms are golden.

## Ingredients

- 400 ml lukewarm milk, about 38 °C
- 1 tbsp sugar
- 7 g (2½ tsp) active dry or instant yeast
- 150 g plain flour
- 100 g buckwheat flour
- A pinch of salt
- 2 large eggs
- Butter, melted, for the pan
- To serve: butter and icing sugar

## Method

1. **Wake the yeast.** Stir the milk, sugar and yeast together and leave for 5–10 minutes, until frothy.
2. **Mix the dry ingredients.** Whisk both flours and the salt in a large bowl.
3. **Make the batter.** Add the yeast mixture and the eggs and whisk until completely smooth; a hand mixer helps.
4. **Let it rise.** Cover and leave in a warm place for about 1 hour, until doubled, light and bubbly.
5. **Heat the pan.** Heat a poffertjes pan over medium heat and brush each hollow generously with melted butter.
6. **Fill and cook.** Pour the batter into a squeeze bottle or piping bag and fill each hollow about three-quarters full. Cook for 1–2 minutes, until the bottoms are golden and bubbles show on top.
7. **Flip.** Turn each one with a skewer or fork and cook for about 1 minute more, until golden.
8. **Serve.** Pile them on a plate while warm, top with a knob of butter and dust heavily with icing sugar.

## Notes

- An aebleskiver pan works instead of a poffertjes pan; the poffertjes come out rounder.
- The note gave cup measures that don’t match its grams (1¾ cups is closer to 220 g of flour), so this uses the grams.
