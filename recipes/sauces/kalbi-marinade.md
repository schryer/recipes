Kiwi does the tenderising here: its enzymes break down the meat fast, so
don't leave short ribs in it for more than a day or they turn mushy.

## Ingredients

- 1 large [onion](/ingredients/onion.md), peeled and roughly chopped
- About 140 g (1 cup) peeled [garlic cloves](/ingredients/garlic.md)
- 1 bunch [scallions](/ingredients/scallion.md) (about 8), roughly chopped
- 5 tbsp [toasted sesame seeds](/ingredients/sesame-seeds.md)
- 200 g (1 cup) [sugar](/ingredients/sugar.md)
- 2 large [kiwifruits](/ingredients/kiwifruit.md)
- 500 ml (2 cups) [soy sauce](/ingredients/soy-sauce.md)
- 250 ml (1 cup) [mirin](/ingredients/mirin.md), [apple juice](/ingredients/apple-juice.md) or [water](/ingredients/water.md)
- 500 ml (2 cups) [fresh orange juice](/ingredients/orange-juice.md)
- 250 ml (1 cup) [toasted sesame oil](/ingredients/sesame-oil.md)

## Method

1. **Load the blender.** Put the onion, garlic, scallions, sesame seeds and sugar in the blender jar.
2. **Add the kiwi.** Halve the kiwifruits, scoop the flesh into the jar and discard the skins.
3. **Blend.** Pour in the soy sauce, mirin, orange juice and sesame oil. Blend on high for about 10 seconds, until smooth.
4. **Marinate.** Use about 500 ml (2 cups) for 1.8 kg (4 lb) flanken-cut short ribs, sliced 1 cm (½ in) thick. Massage it into the meat and refrigerate for at least 2 hours and up to 1 day.
5. **Grill.** Lift the ribs out of the marinade and grill over medium-high heat, about 4 minutes a side, until charred at the edges.

## Notes

- The batch is big: one blender jar makes enough for four rounds of short ribs. Keep the unused marinade in a sealed jar in the fridge for up to a week, or halve the recipe.
- Never reuse marinade that has had raw meat in it.
- It also works for pork shoulder, chicken thighs and thinly sliced rib-eye (bulgogi).
