One of the Kogi truck’s mother sauces: everything goes in the blender at
once. Use unseasoned rice vinegar, or the dressing ends up too sweet.

## Ingredients

- 5 cm (2 in) knob of ginger
- 4 garlic cloves, peeled
- 3 scallions, roughly chopped
- 60 ml (¼ cup) gochugaru (Korean chili flakes)
- 50 g (¼ cup) sugar
- 1 tbsp kosher salt
- 2 tsp black pepper
- 250 ml (1 cup) soy sauce
- 500 ml (2 cups) unseasoned rice vinegar
- 125 ml (½ cup) toasted sesame oil
- 250 ml (1 cup) neutral oil, such as canola or grapeseed

## Method

1. **Peel the ginger.** Scrape off the skin with the edge of a spoon, then slice it into thin coins.
2. **Blend.** Put the ginger and everything else in the blender jar and blend on high for about 15 seconds, until smooth.
3. **Store.** Pour into a sealable jar and refrigerate. Shake before using, as the oil separates.

## Notes

- Keeps for up to 3 months in the fridge, according to the source.
- Toss it through shredded cabbage for Kogi slaw, use it on salads and cold noodles, or brush it over grilled meat and vegetables.
