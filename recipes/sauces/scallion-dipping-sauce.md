A dip for pancakes and dumplings that also works as a glaze or dressing.
Dissolve the sugar before the scallions go in, and slice the scallions
thin so they soften in the sauce.

## Ingredients

- 500 ml (2 cups) [soy sauce](/ingredients/soy-sauce.md)
- 175 ml (¾ cup) [rice vinegar](/ingredients/rice-vinegar.md)
- 2 tbsp [sugar](/ingredients/sugar.md)
- 125 ml (½ cup) [toasted sesame oil](/ingredients/sesame-oil.md)
- 3 tbsp [gochugaru](/ingredients/gochugaru.md) (Korean chili flakes)
- 2 tbsp [toasted sesame seeds](/ingredients/sesame-seeds.md), crushed
- 1 bunch [scallions](/ingredients/scallion.md) (about 8), trimmed and thinly sliced

## Method

1. **Dissolve the sugar.** In a bowl, stir the soy sauce, rice vinegar and sugar until the sugar has dissolved.
2. **Season.** Stir in the sesame oil, gochugaru and crushed sesame seeds.
3. **Add the scallions.** Slice the scallions thinly, in rounds or on a bias, and stir them in.
4. **Rest.** Leave it for 10 minutes so the gochugaru blooms and the scallions soften. Stir before serving, as the oil and chili settle.

## Notes

- Crush the sesame seeds in a mortar, or rub them between your palms, to release their oil.
- Keeps for about a week in a sealed jar in the fridge; the scallions soften further over time.
- Serve with pajeon or mung bean pancakes, dumplings, tofu or grilled meat.
