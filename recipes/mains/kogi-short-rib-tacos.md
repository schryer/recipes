The taco that started the Kogi truck: grilled kalbi crisped on the griddle,
then stacked with three toppings. Make the [Kalbi marinade](../sauces/kalbi-marinade.md),
[Kogi vinaigrette](../sauces/kogi-vinaigrette.md) and [Salsa verde](../sauces/salsa-verde.md)
first, and dress the slaw generously.

## Ingredients

### For the cilantro-onion relish

- ½ medium white [onion](/ingredients/onion.md), finely chopped
- A small handful (¼ cup) [cilantro](/ingredients/coriander.md), finely chopped
- Juice of ½ [lime](/ingredients/lime.md) (about 1 tbsp)
- [Kosher salt](/ingredients/salt.md), to taste
- Freshly ground [black pepper](/ingredients/black-pepper.md), to taste

### For the scallion slaw

- 6 [scallions](/ingredients/scallion.md), trimmed and cut on the diagonal into thin strips
- About 35 g (½ cup) [green cabbage](/ingredients/cabbage.md), thinly sliced
- About 25 g (½ cup) [romaine lettuce](/ingredients/lettuce.md), thinly sliced
- A pinch of [kosher salt](/ingredients/salt.md)
- A pinch of freshly ground [black pepper](/ingredients/black-pepper.md)
- [Kogi vinaigrette](/ingredients/kogi-vinaigrette.md), to taste

### For the tacos

- [Neutral oil](/ingredients/neutral-oil.md), such as canola or grapeseed, to coat the pan
- 8 small [corn tortillas](/ingredients/corn-tortilla.md) (11 cm, 4½ in)
- 450 g (1 lb) cooked [kalbi](/ingredients/kalbi.md), bones removed, chopped into small chunks
- [Salsa verde](/ingredients/salsa-verde.md), to taste
- [Lime](/ingredients/lime.md) wedges, for serving

## Method

1. **Make the relish.** Stir the onion, cilantro and lime juice together in a bowl, season with salt and pepper and set aside.
2. **Make the slaw.** Toss the scallions, cabbage and lettuce in a bowl with the salt and pepper. Add a few large spoonfuls of the vinaigrette and toss gently, adding more until the slaw is well coated and a little dressing pools at the bottom of the bowl.
3. **Warm the tortillas.** Film a large skillet or griddle with oil over medium heat. Cook the tortillas, in batches if needed, about 30 seconds a side, until soft and pliable and just starting to crisp. Keep them warm stacked at the edge of the pan, or under a towel on a plate.
4. **Crisp the kalbi.** Put the chopped kalbi on the hot pan and cook for about 2 minutes, until it caramelises and browns at the edges.
5. **Build the tacos.** Stack two tortillas per taco. Top with the kalbi, spoon over the salsa verde, add the relish and finish with a pile of slaw. Serve straight away with lime wedges.

## Notes

- Vegetarian: use 450 g (1 lb) grilled, roughly chopped mixed vegetables in place of the kalbi. The source pairs them with Roy Choi’s BBQ mixed vegetables, which aren’t on this site yet.
- The relish and slaw are best made just before serving; the slaw wilts as it sits in the dressing.
