Fresh spring rolls that can be rolled ahead or at the table. Soften the
rice paper only briefly: it keeps softening on the board, and soaked too
long it tears.

## Ingredients

### For the rolls

- 50 g [rice vermicelli](/ingredients/rice-vermicelli.md)
- 8 sheets [rice paper](/ingredients/rice-paper.md), about 22 cm (8½ in) across
- 16 cooked [prawns](/ingredients/prawn.md), halved lengthways, or 200 g cooked [chicken](/ingredients/chicken.md) in strips
- ½ [cucumber](/ingredients/cucumber.md), cut into thin sticks
- 1 small bunch [mint](/ingredients/mint.md), leaves picked
- 1 small bunch [coriander](/ingredients/coriander.md), leaves picked

### For the dip

- 4 tbsp [hoisin sauce](/ingredients/hoisin-sauce.md)
- 1 tbsp [peanut butter](/ingredients/peanut-butter.md)
- 2–3 tbsp [warm water](/ingredients/water.md)

## Method

1. **Cook the noodles.** Boil the vermicelli for 3–5 minutes, then drain, rinse under cold water and drain well.
2. **Make the dip.** Stir the hoisin and peanut butter together, then add warm water a spoonful at a time until it pours.
3. **Soften the rice paper.** Dip one sheet in a wide bowl of warm water for about 5 seconds and lay it flat on a damp board.
4. **Fill.** Across the lower third, lay a small handful of noodles, some herbs, cucumber and four prawn halves (cut side up, so they show through).
5. **Roll.** Fold the bottom edge over the filling, fold in the sides, and roll up tightly. Repeat with the rest.
6. **Serve.** Serve straight away with the dip.

## Notes

- To roll at the table, set out the fillings and a bowl of warm water and let everyone make their own.
- Rolls kept under a damp tea towel stay soft for an hour or two; in the fridge the rice paper hardens.
