A southern Vietnamese noodle bowl with no broth to simmer, so it comes
together in the time the noodles take. Slice the beef thin and cook it
fast in a very hot pan so it browns without stewing.

## Ingredients

### For the bowl

- 150 g rice vermicelli
- 2 handfuls shredded lettuce
- 100 g bean sprouts
- 1 small bunch mint, leaves picked
- 1 small bunch coriander, leaves picked
- 2 tbsp crushed roasted peanuts
- 2 tbsp fried shallots

### For the beef

- 300 g beef sirloin or flank, thinly sliced across the grain
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tbsp fish sauce
- 1 tsp sugar
- 1 tbsp neutral oil

### For the sauce

- 150 ml [nước chấm](../sauces/nuoc-cham.md), thinned with water (see Notes)

## Method

1. **Cook the noodles.** Boil the vermicelli for 3–5 minutes, until just tender. Drain, rinse under cold water and drain well.
2. **Season the beef.** Toss the beef with the garlic, fish sauce and sugar.
3. **Fry the beef.** Heat the oil in a wok or large pan over high heat until it shimmers. Add the beef in one layer and stir-fry for about 2 minutes, until browned outside and just cooked.
4. **Build the bowls.** Layer lettuce, bean sprouts and herbs in two bowls, then the noodles, then the beef with its juices.
5. **Serve.** Scatter over the peanuts and fried shallots. Pour the nước chấm over just before eating.

## Notes

- The sauce is the classic dressing of fish sauce, lime juice, sugar, garlic and water. The site’s [nước chấm](../sauces/nuoc-cham.md) is a stronger concentrate: thin about 75 ml of it with 75 ml warm water.
- Fish sauce, rice vermicelli and fried shallots come from an Asian grocer; the herbs, lettuce and beef from any supermarket.
- Lemongrass in the beef makes it bún bò xả (see Similar recipes).
